# Location-aware study suggestions (UW Seattle)

The bot recognizes buildings from a bundled copy of UW's official Seattle-area building footprints. It no longer needs a cafe/library search to identify campus buildings. The catalog contains 303 footprints, including Student Union Building (HUB).

## Try it

1. Stop the existing bot with Ctrl+C, then run `bun start` from `C:\Users\Isabe\my-project`.
2. Keep Find My location shared with the bot's iMessage account.
3. Text `smart prompts on`. This enables building discovery and connects reminders to the demo calendar.
4. Text `where am i` (also `campus location` or `detect building`) for an immediate building estimate from a fresh location. This command does not save a place or start a session, and does not require calendar availability.
5. With smart prompts on, several stable readings at an unfamiliar building can produce a question such as “Looks like you're near Student Union Building (HUB). Save this as a study spot?” Reply yes to save only the place, or skip to dismiss it. Calendar availability is not required for this question.
6. A separate study invitation still requires a 30-minute calendar opening and its own confirmation before starting the linked study session.

`why quiet` shows recent fixed-code decisions. `proactivity status` reports enabled state and saved place categories. `place discovery off` stops discovery of new buildings while preserving saved-place recognition. `smart prompts off` stops reminders. `forget places` removes saved place settings and pending place candidates. Manual `remember place library` (or cafe/home/dorm_room/classroom) remains available outside the campus catalog.

No new secrets, API keys or database migration are required for building recognition. It uses the existing Photon credentials and the local public UW catalog. Study invitations still use existing Google Calendar and xAI connections. Keep one bot process running on an awake computer.

## Building recognition

Source: [UW Seattle campus building layer](https://gis.maps.uw.edu/federated/rest/services/BaseComponents/UW_Seattle_Buildings_and_Paths/FeatureServer/1), queried in GeoJSON with `outSR=4326`, building name, code and geometry. The bundled file records its source, retrieval date and attribution. Only valid polygon/multipolygon features in the Seattle campus vicinity (longitude -122.34 to -122.27, latitude 47.63 to 47.68) are included, with coordinates rounded to six decimal places. This is a snapshot, not a live building inventory; it should be refreshed if UW changes the layer.

**Building data: Engineering Services, University of Washington.** This hackathon uses it for educational purposes. UW provides its map data as-is. Credit UW Engineering Services when presenting the building map or detection results.

Matching happens locally: phone coordinates are not sent to UW, OpenStreetMap or another map provider. Photon remains the source of the phone location. Names/codes come from UW data; they are not generated by a language model. GPS can estimate proximity to a building, but cannot identify a room, floor or whether you are studying.

The matcher uses building footprints rather than only center points. Polygon holes and multiple parts are respected. A point must be within a footprint or no more than 25 meters from it. If its reported accuracy radius also reaches a competing footprint, no building is selected. Building estimates require a valid capture timestamp within two minutes, reported accuracy of 100 meters or better, and a live/shallow Photon source. Stale readings, broad uncertainty, unsupported areas and ambiguous neighboring buildings stay quiet. The explicit location command reports an uncertain result instead of inventing a building.

Automatic confirmation questions additionally require three or more distinct capture timestamps spanning three minutes within the most recent five minutes, with no displacement above 75 meters. Repeated cached snapshots do not establish dwell. Restarting clears this in-memory evidence. Accepting a UW place rechecks a fresh unambiguous match to the same building. Future visits use that footprint ID; they do not use the old generic 200-meter circle. Manual/legacy saved places keep their existing circular geofences.

## Calendar and decision model

Building discovery runs before Calendar lookup and is independent of free gaps, study-window hours and Calendar outages. It still respects explicit enablement, Pacific quiet hours (default 10 PM–8 AM), unfinished sessions/check-ins, outstanding questions, prompt cooldowns and the two-per-day invitation cap. A place-confirmation question uses one of those daily invitations. Skipping pauses prompts for an hour and remembers the dismissed building ID. `where am i` is an explicit request and works even during quiet hours or when proactive limits are reached.

Only study invitations require an eligible opening on Hackathon Demo: at least 30 minutes between commitments, with 10-minute buffers and the 9 AM–8 PM Pacific study window. Grok considers numeric timing/accuracy summaries, the place category and recent accepted/skipped study counts. Coordinates, phone numbers, building names, event titles and chat IDs are excluded from the decision-model input. Grok returns a validated structured offer/stay-quiet decision; unavailable/invalid responses or confidence below 0.75 stay quiet. Confidence and thresholds are heuristics, not calibrated probabilities or proof of availability. Decisions are limited to four per Pacific day with a ten-minute cooldown. Calendar and session state are rechecked before a study invitation and before confirmation. No calendar event is booked automatically.

LinUCB remains the separate study-outcome personalization model. Recognizing a building does not infer productivity, mood or environmental quality and does not retrain a notification model.

## Local storage and testing

Public footprints are in `data/uw-seattle-buildings.json`. Private saved place settings, sender address, pending candidate and reason log are under gitignored `data/proactivity/`; reminder state is also private. Recent raw readings remain in memory only. The log holds at most 100 reason entries; study accepted/skipped history is limited to seven days and 50 entries. Up to 100 dismissed IDs and 30 confirmed places are retained locally.

The old Overpass adapter remains for compatibility/tests, but the running bot uses only the local UW adapter. Its network lookup budgets do not delay local building matching. No actual phone readings or messages are used by automated tests. Tests cover HUB recognition from the official public footprint, polygon holes, ambiguous neighboring buildings, invalid accuracy, recognizing buildings with no calendar openings or with Calendar offline, confirmation after movement, and the separate place-save/study-start flow. Real phone freshness still needs an end-to-end check; a sharing notice or readable cached snapshot does not guarantee continuous refresh.
